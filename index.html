Privacy Policy

SHADOW MAKERS ("we", "us") is a sole proprietorship in British Columbia, Canada, that sells digital products (printables, spreadsheets, planners and clip art) through our Etsy shop. Effective date: October 24, 2026. Contact: contact@shadow-maker-studio.com.

This policy covers three things: this website, our Etsy shop, and a private business tool we use to run the shop. The private tool is used only by the shop owner. It is not a public app, and nobody else signs in to it.

1. This website

This website is a single page hosted on GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub. When you open it, GitHub may collect basic visit information (such as your IP address, the page you view, and your device and browser details) and may keep it in server logs, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The page itself has no forms, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking of our own, and we do not sell anything on it. We do not run a web store here: purchases happen on Etsy.

2. Our Etsy shop and your purchase

You buy on Etsy. Etsy handles your payment and delivery details under Etsy's own policies; we do not receive your card details. Etsy gives the shop information about your order, and our private tool reads our shop's orders from Etsy, with the owner's permission, so we can see how the shop is doing. The tool saves, for each order: the Etsy order number, the date, the order status, the total and currency, and the items bought. An order number and a record of what was bought are information about your purchase, even without your name, because the owner can use the order number to find your order in Etsy.

The tool's order table has no columns for your name, email address or street address, and it does not save them. We use order information to fill orders, answer questions about them, keep business and tax records, and see which products sell. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Custom requests

If you ask for a custom item, the owner types a description of your idea into the tool. The tool keeps that description, your country (to price shipping), and the pictures made for the request, on the owner's computer. The tool does not store your name, address or contact details. If you send a picture of your own and the owner uploads it, the tool stores a copy.

4. Pinterest

We use Pinterest to show our own products. With the owner's permission through Pinterest's sign-in (OAuth), our private tool can:

  • read the owner's Pinterest username, account type, and the names and IDs of the owner's boards;
  • create a Pin, with a picture, title and description about one of our own Etsy listings, on one of the owner's boards.

A Pin is created only after the owner has looked at that exact Pin and approved it. The tool does not read, collect or store any other Pinterest user's information, does not follow, message or comment for anyone, does not use Pinterest data for advertising or profiling, and we do not share or sell it. We store the sign-in tokens Pinterest gives us, the username and account type, the chosen board, and a record of each Pin (its text, picture, link and Pinterest's Pin ID). The tokens are kept in a file only the owner's user account on that computer can read, outside the project folder; the file is protected by that account and its file permissions but is not additionally encrypted. The owner can disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored tokens from that computer, and can remove access on Pinterest under Settings > Security. Pinterest's own privacy policy applies to your use of Pinterest.

5. Google Drive, AI services and email

  • Google Drive. To have a print-on-demand partner print some products, the tool uploads the print file to a Drive folder it creates and shares that one file by link so the partner can download it. The permission is limited to files the tool itself creates; the tool cannot see the rest of the owner's Drive. When a file is no longer needed it is moved to Drive's trash; the tool never permanently deletes anything.
  • AI services. We use AI services to help write product wording (Ollama's cloud service) and to make pictures (Google's Gemini). They receive the product ideas and descriptions the owner writes, including, for a custom request, the owner's description of your idea. They are not sent buyers' names, email addresses or order numbers.
  • Print-on-demand. Gelato receives orders for print-on-demand items through Etsy. Our tool only asks Gelato for price quotes, using a placeholder address, never a buyer's.
  • Email. The tool sends status emails from a Google (Gmail) account to the owner's own mailbox. They are designed not to include customer details.

6. Other service providers

Etsy; Pinterest; Google; Ollama; Gelato; EverBee (Etsy market research from public listing data, not customer data); GitHub (hosts this web page). Each handles information under its own policy.

7. Where it is kept, and for how long

The tool runs on computers the owner controls, with a local database and files. Only the owner can use the tool, and its dashboard needs a passphrase. No storage method is perfectly secure, and we make no guarantee.

The tool does not delete records automatically. Order records, custom-request records, Pin records, product files and log files stay on the owner's computers until the owner deletes them by hand, and Etsy, Pinterest and Google keep their own records under their own policies. We keep order and bookkeeping records for as long as we need them for the shop's business and tax records. The tool does not make backups of its data automatically. Log files are not rotated automatically. We may change these practices and will update this policy if we do.

8. Your choices

Email contact@shadow-maker-studio.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. Your payment and delivery details are held by Etsy; contact Etsy for those. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
